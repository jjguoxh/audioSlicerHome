Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

AudioSlicer is a local offline audio processing tool for macOS. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our application and official website.

We prioritize user privacy. All audio files you process remain stored locally on your Mac and will never be uploaded to any cloud server without your explicit consent.

2. Data We Collect

  • Local App Settings: The app stores preferences, export quota counters and interface configurations on your device only. This data never leaves your Mac.
  • Feedback Form Information: If you submit feedback via the website form, you may voluntarily provide your email address and text content. We only use this email to reply to your questions or suggestions.
  • No Audio Uploads: SACD-ISO, DSF, CUE, FLAC, MP3, M4A and other audio files are processed entirely offline. We do not read, copy or upload any music files to external servers.
  • No Tracking & Analytics: The app and official website do not embed third-party tracking tools, advertising SDKs or statistical scripts to collect your browsing or usage behavior.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Local configuration data is only used to retain your personalized app settings between launches.
  • Your submitted feedback email will only be used for one-on-one communication regarding your feedback. We will not send marketing emails or share your email with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

All local app data is stored within macOS App Sandbox with system-level protection. Feedback submissions are temporarily stored on third-party form service with encryption and will not be disclosed to unrelated parties.

5. Your Rights

  • You may delete the app at any time to erase all local configuration data stored on your device.
  • To delete your submitted feedback records, contact our support email with your feedback content and email address for identity verification.
  • You are not required to provide any personal information to use the core playback features of AudioSlicer.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: Used only to verify in-app purchase status, governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • Website feedback form: Powered by a third-party form service to receive user messages.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please send an email to our support address via the feedback page on this website.

一、概述

AudioSlicer 是一款 macOS 本地离线音频处理工具。本隐私政策说明您在使用本应用及官方网站时，我们收集、使用与保护您信息的规则。

我们高度重视用户隐私安全：您处理的所有音频文件仅保存在本地 Mac，未经您明确许可，绝不会上传至任何云端服务器。

二、我们收集的数据

  • 本地应用配置：软件仅在您设备本地存储偏好设置、导出额度计数、界面配置，该数据不会离开您的电脑。
  • 反馈表单信息：若您通过官网反馈表单提交留言，可自愿填写邮箱与反馈文字。邮箱仅用于回复您的问题与功能建议。
  • 不上传任何音频：SACD-ISO、DSF、CUE、FLAC、MP3、M4A 等音频全程本地离线解析转换，不会读取、复制或上传音乐文件至外部服务器。
  • 无追踪统计：应用与官网未嵌入第三方埋点、广告、行为统计脚本，不采集您的使用、浏览行为。

三、数据使用方式

  • 本地配置数据仅用于保存您的个性化软件设置，重启应用后生效。
  • 您提交反馈留下的邮箱仅用于一对一沟通问题，不会发送营销邮件，也不会转交任何第三方。

四、数据存储与安全

本地软件数据存放于 macOS 应用沙盒，受系统权限保护；网站反馈内容加密临时存储于第三方表单服务，不会泄露给无关第三方。

五、您的权利

  • 您可随时卸载软件，清除全部本地存储的配置数据。
  • 如需删除您提交的反馈记录，可通过官网反馈页面发送邮件，提供对应邮箱与留言内容核验身份后处理。
  • 使用 AudioSlicer 全部播放核心功能无需提供任何个人信息。

六、第三方服务

  • Apple StoreKit：仅用于校验内购购买状态，受苹果隐私政策约束。
  • 网站反馈表单：依托第三方表单服务接收用户留言。

七、联系我们

如对本隐私政策存在疑问，可通过本网站反馈页面发送邮件联系开发者。
